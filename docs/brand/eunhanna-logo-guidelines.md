# EUNHANNA Logo Guidelines

## Recommended mark

Concept A (Orbit) is the production mark. Its complete rounded silhouette remains recognizable at favicon size, while the three route lines and destination node imply an `E` without turning the symbol into a literal letter badge.

## Construction

- Primary blue: `#3B82F6`
- Dark wordmark: `#111827`
- Reverse: white wordmark on dark surfaces
- Clear space: at least 25% of the icon diameter on every side
- Minimum icon size: 16px digital, 6mm print
- Minimum horizontal lockup width: 120px digital, 32mm print

## Usage

- Use `eunhanna-horizontal.svg` on light surfaces.
- Use `eunhanna-horizontal-white.svg` on dark surfaces.
- Use `eunhanna-horizontal-black.svg` when only one ink is available.
- Use `eunhanna-icon.svg` for favicons, avatars, app icons, and compact navigation.

Do not sharpen corners, add arrows or speed lines, distort proportions, rotate the mark, recolor individual route lines, add shadows/glows, or place the mark on a background with insufficient contrast.

## Concept rationale

1. **Orbit (recommended):** enclosed rounded silhouette, strongest small-size recognition.
2. **Converge:** three routes meet at a destination, most explicit navigation story.
3. **Loop:** continuous route forms an implied E, most minimal and abstract.
